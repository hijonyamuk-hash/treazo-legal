1. Registration and legal bases

Treazo is an app for families that helps parents manage pocket money, tasks and screen time. We process personal data only as far as this is necessary to provide the app.

  • Sign in with Apple. If you register with Sign in with Apple, we receive the identifier Apple provides to us and, if you choose to share it, your name and an email address (which may be a private relay address). Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Registration with email. If you register with an email address, we process your email address and the password you set in order to create and secure your account. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Purchases and subscriptions. Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether a subscription is active. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and our legitimate interest in correct subscription handling (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Server security logs. To operate and protect our servers, technical logs are created automatically. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in the secure operation of the service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Child profiles. Child profiles are created exclusively by parents or legal guardians. Children do not register on their own. The guardian is responsible for the child's use of the app. Legal basis: performance of the contract with the guardian (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Push notifications. If you allow notifications, we store a device token issued by Apple (APNs) so that we can deliver notifications to the right device, for example when a task is submitted, when a parent approves extra time or when a parent sets a screen-time lock. You can switch notifications off at any time in the iOS settings. Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

2. Categories of data we process

  • Identifiers: user ID, name or nickname of the profiles, email address (if provided), Apple sign-in identifier, push notification device token.
  • App data: family and profile information, tasks and routines, rewards, balances and transaction history, savings goals, wish lists, loans and advances, settings.
  • Screen-time data: see section 3 below.
  • Technical data: IP address, access timestamps and error logs on our servers, confirmation timestamps that a parent's lock has arrived on the child's device.

We do not use advertising identifiers, we do not show third-party advertising and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

3. Screen Time and parental controls

Treazo lets parents set screen-time rules for their own children, for example daily limits, school and bedtime windows, a hard limit for selected apps, a "lock now" function, and extra time that children can earn by completing tasks. For this, Treazo uses Apple's Screen Time technologies (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity). Apple shows its own permission dialog, and the rules only apply after a parent has granted this permission on the child's device.

What we store in your family account:

  • The screen-time rules set by parents: daily or weekly time budget, school and bedtime windows, hard-limit minutes, whether a lock is active and when it was set, and a temporary unlock period.
  • The selection of apps and categories that parents allow, limit or block. These selections are stored as opaque tokens issued by Apple. Treazo cannot use them to see which apps your child opens or how often.
  • Unlock codes (PIN) that parents set so a child can ask for or receive extra time.
  • Requests for extra time: the child's profile, the number of minutes requested and granted, the display name of the app the request is about, the status of the request (for example approved or declined) and timestamps.
  • Screen time earned through tasks (number of minutes and validity), shown to parents and children in the app.
  • Confirmations that a lock set by a parent has arrived on the child's device (type of lock and timestamps).

What stays on the device: Usage statistics (for example how long an app was used today) are calculated and shown by Apple's Screen Time on the device. Treazo does not send them to its servers and does not read the content of other apps.

Purpose and legal basis: We process this data only to provide the screen-time features that the parents have set up (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We do not use it for advertising, profiling or tracking.

Notifications for locks: If a parent locks a device and the child's device does not confirm the lock, Treazo sends a visible push notification to the child's device so the lock can take effect. For this we use the device token described in section 1.

Parents decide. Screen-time rules can only be set by parents or legal guardians. You can switch them off or delete them at any time in the app, or revoke the permission in the iOS Screen Time settings. Where children are concerned, parents are responsible for informing them about these rules in an age-appropriate way.
