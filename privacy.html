1. The most important points

  • Treazo is an app for families: pocket money, tasks, savings goals and screen time. There is no real bank account and we do not process bank or card data. All balances in Treazo are virtual.
  • We show no advertising, we do not pass on data for advertising purposes, and we use no advertising identifier and no tracking across other apps or websites. We do not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • Child profiles are created exclusively by parents or legal guardians. Children do not register on their own.
  • Screen-time usage data (for example how long an app was used) stays on the device and is not sent to us.
  • You can delete your account or your whole family in the app at any time.

2. Registration, account and family

  • Sign in with Apple. We receive the identifier Apple provides to us and, if you choose to share it, your name and an email address (which may be an Apple relay address). Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Registration with email and password. We process your email address and a password, which is stored only as a hash value. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Family and profiles. A family contains profiles for adults and children (name or nickname, role, family code, settings and a profile password or PIN, stored only as a hash value). Several adults can join with the family code. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Face ID / Touch ID. Biometric unlocking is performed entirely by iOS. We neither receive nor store biometric data.

3. Children

Treazo is aimed at parents who support their children. Child profiles are created exclusively by parents or legal guardians. They decide which data is entered and consent to the processing on behalf of the child. Children do not provide an email address or payment data to us. We do not use children's data for advertising, profiling or tracking. Please inform your child in an age-appropriate way about which data is visible in the app, in particular about screen-time rules.

5. Screen time and parental controls

Treazo lets parents set screen-time rules for their own children, for example daily or weekly limits, school and bedtime windows, a hard limit for selected apps, a "lock now" function, and extra time that children can earn by completing tasks. For this, Treazo uses Apple's Screen Time technologies (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity). Apple shows its own permission dialog, and the rules only apply after a parent has granted this permission on the child's device.

What we store in your family account:

  • the rules set by parents: daily or weekly time budget, school and bedtime windows, hard-limit minutes, whether a lock is active and when it was set, and a temporary unlock period,
  • the selection of apps and categories that parents allow, limit or block. These selections are stored as opaque tokens issued by Apple. Treazo cannot use them to see which apps your child opens or how often,
  • unlock codes (PIN) that parents set so that a child can ask for or receive extra time,
  • requests for extra time: the child's profile, the number of minutes requested and granted, the display name of the app the request is about, the status of the request (for example approved or declined) and timestamps,
  • screen time earned through tasks (minutes and validity),
  • confirmations that a lock set by a parent has arrived on the child's device (type of lock and timestamps),
  • whether the Screen Time permission has been granted on the device and since when.

What stays on the device: Usage statistics (for example how long an app was used today) are calculated and displayed by Apple's Screen Time on the device. Treazo does not send them to its servers and does not read the content of other apps.

Purpose and legal basis: We process this data only to provide the screen-time features that parents have set up (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We do not use it for advertising, profiling or tracking.

For locks: If a parent sets a lock and the child's device does not confirm it, Treazo sends a visible push notification to the child's device so that the lock can take effect (see section 6).

Parents decide. Only parents or legal guardians can set screen-time rules. You can switch them off or delete them in the app at any time, or revoke the permission in the iOS Screen Time settings.

6. Push notifications and reminders

If you allow notifications, we store a device token issued by Apple (APNs) so that we can deliver notifications to the right device, for example when a task is submitted or approved, when extra screen time is requested or granted, or when a lock is set. A token always belongs to the currently active profile. In addition, the app schedules local reminders on your device (for example for due tasks). You can switch notifications off at any time in the iOS settings. Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

7. Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive payment data from Apple. To check whether a subscription is active, we use RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous user identifier and information about the purchase and the device for this purpose. We store the subscription status and the expiry date in your account. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and our legitimate interest in correct subscription management (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Subscriptions renew automatically unless they are cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You manage and cancel them in your Apple ID settings. In addition, Apple's standard End User License Agreement applies.

8. Technical data and server logs

When the app is operated, technical data is created, for example IP address, time of access and error messages. It serves the secure operation of the service and error correction. Server logs are deleted after 7 days at the latest. Legal basis: legitimate interest in secure operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

9. Recipients and service providers

  • Supabase (hosting, database, authentication). The servers are located in Frankfurt am Main (EU). A data processing agreement is in place with the provider.
  • RevenueCat (subscription check). Data may be transferred to the USA. This is based on standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).
  • Apple processes data under its own responsibility, for example for Sign in with Apple, purchases, push notifications, Family Sharing and Screen Time. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

We do not pass data on to any further third parties, in particular not for advertising purposes.

10. Storage period and deletion

We store account and family data as long as the account exists. In the app, under Delete accounts & data permanently, you can choose whether to delete only your own account or the whole family with all data. Server logs are deleted after 7 days at the latest. Purchase receipts are kept for as long as tax and commercial law require. Feedback messages are deleted once they have been answered and are no longer needed.

11. Storage on your device

The app stores settings and cached data on your device, for example language, the last active profile, a queue for changes made offline and the screen-time configuration. They are stored in the app and in a shared app group with the Screen Time extensions and are removed when you delete the app.

12. Data security

Data is transmitted between the app and our servers in encrypted form (TLS). Passwords and PINs are stored only as hash values. Access to data is restricted to family members. No service is completely secure; please protect your device and your credentials.

13. No automated decision-making

We do not make decisions that are based solely on automated processing and have legal effect on you. All rules (rewards, locks, limits) are set by the parents themselves.
